RULE EVERYONE INTERACTING ONLINE MUST FOLLOW

1.Respect others online

- Dont insult or bully anyone online.

2.Protect your personal information

- Never share passwords.pins or id number online.

3.Think before posting and commenting

- Make sure your words are respectful ,true,and will not hurt
or harm others,once posted online it cannot be easily taken back.

4.Do not spread fake news

- Always verify information before sharing.

5.Use strong passwords

- Combine letters, numbers, and symbols.

6.Do not click suspicious links

- They may contain scams or viruses.

7.Respect privacy

- Do not share someones videos,photo without permission.

8.Avoid online scums

- If an offer sounds too good just know it is fake.

9.Be polite in comments and chats

- Some comments are too heavy for others to carry.

10.Report abuse or fraud

- Use platform reporting tools when neccessary.

11.Keep software updated

- Updates help to protect against security threats.

12.Log out in shared devices

- Especially in cyber cafes and public computers.

13.Do not impersonate others

- Fake accounts can cause harm and trouble.

14.Limit screen time

- Balance online life with real life.

15.Follow online laws

- To keep online safe this laws must be followed.
